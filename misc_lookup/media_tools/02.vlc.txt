##make a clip in vlc

1. How to Record a Section in VLC
    • Open VLC Media Player and go to the top menu.
    • Click View and select Advanced Controls to show the extra buttons above the normal play controls.
    • Move the slider to the start point of the section you want to clip.
    • Click the red Record button and hit Play.
    • Let the video play until you reach the end point of your desired section.
    • Click the red Record button again to stop recording.
    • Find your new clip automatically saved inside your computer's default Videos folder. 
	

2. Using the Convert/Save method for precise second-by-second cutting

How to Cut Videos Precisely via Convert/Save
    1. Open VLC and press Ctrl + R (Windows) or Cmd + Shift + W (Mac) to open the Open Media window.
    2. Click Add to import your .mp4 video file.
    3. Check the Show more options box at the bottom left of the window.
    4. Locate the Start Time field and enter your desired start time in seconds (e.g., enter 120 for 2 minutes).
    5. Locate the Stop Time field right below it and enter your desired end time in seconds.
    6. Click the arrow next to the Play button at the bottom and choose Convert (or press Alt + O).
    7. Select your preferred output profile (like Video - H.264 + MP3 (MP4)) in the settings.
    8. Click Browse to name your new clip and choose a saving location.
    9. Click Start to process and save your precise video segment.
